# Client Handover Checklist

- Final logo and brand approval
- Founder and co-founder photographs
- Exact Google Maps location
- Final business email
- Confirmed bank/NBFC/insurer relationships and logo permissions
- Approved English, Hindi and Marathi copy
- Approved privacy policy, terms and disclaimer
- Verified service eligibility, documents, charges and process details
- Final domain DNS connection
- Resend sending-domain verification
- Google Analytics and Search Console access
- Cross-device and form testing sign-off
